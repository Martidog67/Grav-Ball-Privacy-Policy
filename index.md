# Privacy Policy for Grav Ball

**Last updated: August 2, 2026**

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Grav Ball** ("the app") handles, and why. The app is developed and published by Daniel Powell ("we", "us").

Grav Ball is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no login, no chat, and no social features. We do not run any servers of our own, and we never receive your game data.

---

## The short version

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- Your game progress stays on your device. We never see it.
- The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device information in order to serve them.
- In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland you are asked to consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in the app's Settings.
- On iOS you are separately asked whether the app may track you. You can say no and the game works exactly the same.

---

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device's standard app storage:

- Your best distance and total coins
- Which ball, map, trail, and music track you own and have equipped
- Which levels you have completed
- Your difficulty and sound, haptics, and trail settings
- Lifetime play statistics used for in-game achievements (runs played, distance travelled, coins earned, gravity wells drawn)

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently, and it cannot be recovered.

---

## Advertising

Grav Ball displays ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. Ads appear between some runs, and you can optionally choose to watch an ad in exchange for in-game coins or to continue a run. Watching those optional ads is always your choice.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- Advertising identifiers (such as the iOS IDFA or Android Advertising ID)
- Device information, such as model, operating system version, and language
- General location inferred from your IP address (country or region level, not a precise position)
- Interactions with ads, such as views and taps

We do not receive this information. We only see anonymous, aggregated reports showing totals such as how many ads were shown and how much revenue they earned. Those reports cannot identify you.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

---

## Your choices about ads

**If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland**, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, asking whether ads may be personalized. You can change your answer at any time:

> Open the app → **Settings** → **Ad privacy settings**

If you decline personalized ads, ads will still appear, but they will be non-personalized and based only on general context rather than your device's advertising identifier.

**On iOS**, Apple separately asks for permission to track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the app does not use your advertising identifier, and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this later in your device's **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

**On any device**, you can reset or limit your advertising identifier through your system settings:

- iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising
- Android: Settings → Google → Ads

## Children

Grav Ball is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will look into it. Because the app collects no personal information and stores no data off the device, there is generally nothing for us to delete beyond what removing the app already erases.

---

## Data we do not collect

To be explicit, the app does not collect, store, or transmit:

- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
- Your precise location
- Any account credentials

The app does not use analytics services beyond the reporting built into Google AdMob.

---

## Security

Because game data stays on your device and we operate no servers, there is no database of user information for us to lose. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode and the operating system's app sandboxing.

---

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as the right to access, correct, or delete it, or to object to processing.

Since we hold no personal data about you, most of these rights are satisfied by actions you can take directly:

- **Delete everything we could relate to you**: uninstall the app, or use **Settings → Reset progress** inside it.
- **Withdraw consent to personalized ads**: **Settings → Ad privacy settings**.
- **Requests concerning data held by Google**: contact Google directly using the policies linked above, as they are the controller of that data.

If you have a question we can help with, contact us using the details below.
